## Toyota Racing Development USA | Materials Lab & Failure Analysis

During my summer internship at Toyota Racing Development (TRD), I worked with the Materials Lab and Failure Analysis Group, supporting laboratory operations and contributing to projects involving materials characterization, failure analysis, and experimental testing.

A major part of my internship involved assisting with failure analysis of engine components. I helped prepare and examine metallographic mounts, document findings, and analyze fracture surfaces and other physical characteristics to better understand potential failure mechanisms. This gave me hands-on experience with the process of investigating component failures and connecting physical evidence to potential causes.

I also supported material characterization and testing, collecting and organizing measurements from engine components and using the results to evaluate material properties and consistency. Through this work, I gained experience with laboratory testing, data collection, and technical documentation.

My second major project focused on recommissioning and standardizing a rotating disc electrode optical emission spectrometer (RDE-OES) used for analyzing engine oil. I researched the testing process, helped establish procedures, performed initial testing and troubleshooting, and analyzed test results to evaluate the consistency and reliability of the system. I also developed standardized documentation and work instructions to help establish a repeatable testing process. This project gave me experience with experimental planning, troubleshooting, data analysis, and developing procedures for laboratory work.

Overall, my internship provided hands-on experience at the intersection of materials science, failure analysis, laboratory testing, and engine development. I gained a better understanding of how engineering analysis is applied in an industry setting while developing practical skills in experimental testing, data analysis, technical documentation, and laboratory work.
